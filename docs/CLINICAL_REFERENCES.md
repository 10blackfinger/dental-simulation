# Clinical reference index and content boundaries

This index records the professional sources used to frame the educational pathways and the local AI assistant prompt. It is a navigation list and concise editorial summary, not a reproduction of the guideline text. The application does not bundle full-text copies of these publications.

## Main sources

### Acute dental problems — SDCEP, second edition, March 2026
- Official guide: https://www.acutedentalproblems.sdcep.org.uk/
- Publisher note: https://sdcep.org.uk/published-guidance/acute-dental-problems/
- The second edition covers pain, swelling, bleeding, trauma, ulceration, altered sensation and abnormal appearance in the head and neck, with urgency and care-provider pathways.
- New supporting tools were announced on 30 September 2026: https://sdcep.org.uk/news/new-supporting-tools-for-the-sdcep-management-of-acute-dental-problems-guidance/
- Application use: acute problems and emergency-triage learning pathway. The UI intentionally stays at the level of recognition, triage and referral principles rather than providing a step-by-step emergency treatment protocol.

### Drug prescribing for dentistry — SDCEP
- Official site: https://www.sdcepdentalprescribing.nhs.scot/
- Publisher updates: https://sdcep.org.uk/published-guidance/drug-prescribing/
- The site notes consistency with BNF 92 and BNFC 2026–27, and that the bacterial-infections section was updated in May 2026.
- Jurisdiction: this is UK prescribing guidance. It must not be treated as the Egyptian formulary or as a substitute for current local law, medicine labels and institutional policy.
- Application use: reference link and AI grounding only. The app does not give drug doses or patient-specific prescriptions.

### Caries management — American Dental Association
- Official guideline hub: https://www.ada.org/resources/research/science/evidence-based-dental-research/caries-management-clinical-practice-guidelines
- The ADA hub lists restorative caries treatment recommendations published in 2023 and nonrestorative treatment recommendations published in 2018.
- Application use: case pathway covering assessment, restorability, isolation, material selection and reassessment. The pathway does not prescribe cavity dimensions, etching times or material-specific operating parameters.

### Endodontics — American Association of Endodontists
- Guidelines and position statements: https://www.aae.org/specialty/clinical-resources/guidelines-position-statements/
- Includes resources on vital pulp therapy, case difficulty assessment, dental dams, CBCT use and endodontic treatment standards.
- Application use: diagnostic reasoning, case difficulty, anatomical understanding, isolation and follow-up. It does not provide file sizes, working lengths, irrigant concentrations or procedural parameters.

### Periodontitis — European Federation of Periodontology
- Stage I–III guideline: https://www.efp.org/education/continuing-education/clinical-guidelines/guideline-on-treatment-of-stage-i-iii-periodontitis/
- The EFP describes the guideline as evidence-based and containing 62 recommendations related to stage I–III periodontitis under the 2018 classification.
- Application use: periodontal assessment, risk-factor control, non-surgical treatment concepts, reassessment and supportive care.

### Dental trauma — International Association of Dental Traumatology
- Official guideline page: https://iadt-dentaltrauma.org/guidelines-and-resources/guidelines/
- The page provides the 2020 guidelines for fractures/luxations, avulsion of permanent teeth and injuries in the primary dentition, with Arabic among the listed translations.
- Application use: distinguish injury types and primary/permanent dentition, document the injury, use the appropriate guideline and plan follow-up. The simulator does not automate trauma treatment.

### Software clinical decision support — US FDA
- Overview: https://www.fda.gov/medical-devices/digital-health-center-excellence/step-6-software-function-intended-provide-clinical-decision-support
- The FDA describes the importance of allowing a health professional to independently review the basis of recommendations and not rely primarily on software for a patient-specific clinical decision.
- Application use: the AI assistant is positioned as educational reference and explanation, not autonomous diagnosis, treatment planning or time-critical decision support. Applicable regulation depends on intended use and jurisdiction; this page is not Egyptian legal advice.

## Clinical content policy in this project

- Scenarios are educational frameworks for supervised discussion. They are not complete treatment recipes, clinical protocols or skill-certification assessments.
- The app avoids patient-specific diagnosis, drug selection/dosing, emergency treatment instructions and unreviewed invasive procedural parameters.
- Dental guidelines change. Users must open and verify the latest original source and follow their own country's regulations and institution's protocols.
- UK SDCEP recommendations are explicitly labelled UK context and are not automatically transferable to Egypt.
- Anatomy assets may contain estimated or schematic structures. A public dataset licence does not establish clinical accuracy.
- Any clinical release requires review by qualified clinicians for the relevant specialties, including restorative dentistry, endodontics, oral surgery, periodontology, prosthodontics, orthodontics, paediatric dentistry and dental emergencies.

## Known model limitations requiring specialist review

- The BodyParts3D-derived oral model contains 28 permanent teeth and does not represent the full dentition or all soft tissues.
- Dental Scope expands the atlas to 32 permanent teeth and additional tissues, but its source explicitly labels some layers, landmarks and neurovascular branches as schematic or estimated.
- The full-body Écorché atlas is one adult male anatomy model and is not a diagnostic anatomical reference.
- The human patient GLB characters are generic educational assets; fixed horizontal display is not evidence of correct clinical positioning or biomechanics.
- Instruments and materials are simplified props, not physical digital twins or verified manufacturer models.

### Pediatric dentistry — American Academy of Pediatric Dentistry (AAPD), Reference Manual 2026–2027
- Official recommendations hub: https://www.aapd.org/research/oral-health-policies--recommendations/
- Pulp therapy for primary and immature permanent teeth, latest revision 2026: https://www.aapd.org/research/oral-health-policies--recommendations/pulp-therapy-for-primary-and-immature-permanent-teeth/
- Caries-risk assessment and management for infants, children and adolescents, latest revision 2022: https://www.aapd.org/research/oral-health-policies--recommendations/caries-risk-assessment-and-management-for-infants-children-and-adolescents/
- Behavior guidance for the pediatric dental patient, latest revision 2024: https://www.aapd.org/research/oral-health-policies--recommendations/behavior-guidance-for-the-pediatric-dental-patient/
- Application use: pediatric pathway references for age-appropriate communication, caries-risk assessment, primary/immature permanent tooth pulp assessment and behavior guidance. The app does not reproduce the guideline protocols or provide sedation/anesthesia instructions.
- Jurisdiction: AAPD recommendations are US professional guidance. Verify local Egyptian law, institutional policy and current specialist recommendations before clinical use.
